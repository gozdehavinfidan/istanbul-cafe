About

Classic Turkish cooking, made for sharing

Our menu follows the everyday cooking of Istanbul: meze with warm pita, kebabs from the grill, slow-cooked gyro wrapped in lavash, and baklava to finish.

Stop in for a wrap at lunch, or gather friends around a mezze platter and a round of Turkish tea.

  • Meze Hummus, baba ganoush, dolmas and falafel, served with warm pita. View starters
  • From the grill Adana and shish kebabs, gyro and the mixed grill, with rice and salad. View plates
  • To finish Baklava and Turkish delight, with Turkish tea or coffee. View desserts

Starters & Appetizers

Most are served with warm pita bread.

Hummus

A silky dip of garbanzo beans with garlic, tahini, olive oil, and lemon juice. Served with pita bread.

Baba Ganoush

Charbroiled eggplant dip with fresh herbs, tahini, yogurt, olive oil, and lemon juice. Served with pita bread.

Cacikli (Tzatziki)

Yogurt with shredded cucumber, fresh diced dill, and a hint of garlic. Served with pita bread.

Shakshuka

A Mediterranean appetizer of tomatoes, peppers, and eggplant. Served with pita bread.

Dolmas

Tender vine leaves rolled with rice, pine nuts, onion, currants, and fresh herbs. Served with pita bread.

Falafel

Chickpea falafel, pan-fried and served with a side of hummus, with tahini sauce on top.

For sharing

Appetizers Combo

A mezze platter of traditional Mediterranean meze: hummus, tzatziki, baba ganoush, shakshuka, dolmas, and falafel. Served with pita bread.

Adana Kebab Wrap
